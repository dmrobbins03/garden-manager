# Longan (Dimocarpus longan) - research note, Pasadena CA (2026-11-19)

**Agreement across sources.** Longan wants full sun, well-drained soil "on the acidic side", protection while young from frost and from hot dry wind, and no waterlogging or salty water. Fruit does not ripen off the tree; harvest whole clusters when tan and sweet. Kohala is the standard cultivar in California and bears erratically.

**Key numbers and origin**
- Cold (UF/IFAS, Florida): young trees severely damaged at 29-31F, may die at 26-28F; mature trees injured at 25-26F. UC: hardened mature trees take about 22F briefly. App threshold set to 32F.
- Fertilizer (UF/IFAS): 6-6-6 type, 0.5-0.75 lb per application, 4-5 times a year in years 2-3; no nitrogen after August. Dates adapted: 03-15, 05-01, 06-15, 08-01.
- Iron (UF/IFAS): chelate drench 0.5-0.75 oz in 4-5 gal, 2-3 times a year; young-tree total 0.75-1.5 oz/yr. EDDHA for pH above 7.5 and spring timing from USU. Dates: 04-15, 06-15, 08-15.
- Minor elements (UF/IFAS): 3 foliar sprays a year (Mn, Zn, Mg, B, Mo), April-September. Dates: 04-20, 06-01, 09-01.
- Size/spacing (UC Ventura): 15-25 ft tall in California, 15-25 ft spacing.
- Bearing: 2-3 years air-layered (UF/IFAS, UC), 3-5 years (UC Ventura), seedlings up to 6.

**California versus Florida.** Florida harvest is mid-July to early September. UC lists Kohala as "Fall"; UC Ventura gives late July-December depending on location; a south Orange County grower reports late September at the earliest, with cool Octobers often spoiling ripening. Harvest window recorded as 09-15 to 11-15 (estimate). Florida's autumn dry-down for flowering is noted only for bearing-age trees.

**Disagreements.** Mature cold tolerance (22F vs 24-26F); years to bearing; UF/IFAS per-drench iron rate times frequency exceeds its own yearly table for young trees (used the lower figure); UF/IFAS lists many pests while UC Ventura calls longan essentially pest-free in California.

**Unverified.** Southern California flowering dates; a longan-specific heat threshold (100F is general heat-wave guidance); watering intervals; numeric pH (5.5-7.0 from a nursery only); California insect pests; the CRFG longan page could not be opened.
