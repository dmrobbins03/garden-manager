# Japanese maple (Acer palmatum, cultivar unknown) - research note, Pasadena 10b

## What sources agree on
- **Marginal climate.** UC IPM says maples are poorly suited to southern/desert regions; Sunset says filtered shade and shelter from hot, dry or constant wind in California.
- **Scorch is mostly water and wind, not just sun.** UC MG Placer and UCCE Ventura: brown edges come from water deficit, dry wind, and salts from hard water or fertilizer.
- **Deep, steady moisture, never soggy.** UC IPM and Sunset; UC MG Placer names overwatering as a leading cause of death.
- **Salts.** Sunset: occasional flooding leaches salts. UC IPM: leach with extra water, mulch, minimize fertilizer.

## Key numbers
- pH 5.5-6.5 (Roger's Gardens, Mr Maple); iron deficiency above about pH 7.0-7.5 (UC IPM).
- Mulch 3 in, off the trunk (UC MG Placer).
- Established watering every 7-10 days (Roger's); once or twice a week in summer dry spells (Mendocino Maples).
- Size: slow to 20 ft, equal or greater spread (Sunset).
- Frost: 32 F, new growth only (Mendocino Maples). Heat: 85 F onset of stress in trees and shrubs (Rutgers, general).
- Pasadena water hardness 12.9 grains/gal, "very hard" (PWP).

## Disagreements
- **Pruning time.** Sunset: summer or early fall in mild-winter areas (less sap bleed). Mendocino: mid-July to August. Pacific Horticulture and Mr Maple: winter dormancy for structure, avoid full summer heat. Chosen for inland heat: Dec 15 - Jan 31, light touch-ups in June.
- **Feeding.** UC IPM: do not fertilize established woody plants without a shown deficiency. Sunset and nurseries: light periodic feeding. Recorded as one optional light feed.
- **Gypsum.** Roger's recommends it for salts; UC IPM says amendments do not fix saline soil.

## Unverified
- Heat threshold specific to Japanese maple (95 F may be a better alert here).
- Watering depth/gallons; fertilizer amount for a 6 ft tree.
- All calendar dates (03-15 feed, 04-15 chelate, 05-01 mulch, 06-15 and 09-01 leach) and the Pasadena dormancy dates are fitted to local climate, not sourced.
- Chlorine/chloramine harm; best chelate form; shot hole borer risk.
